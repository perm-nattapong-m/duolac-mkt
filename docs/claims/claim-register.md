## Status definitions

- verified: supported by an official or approved source and cleared for the relevant channel.
- pending_verification: research lead only; do not publish.
- restricted: usable only with specific wording, context, rights, or disclaimer.
- not_for_publication: internal reference; never use in generated customer-facing content.

## High-risk claim types

Always require review for medical, disease-related, guaranteed-outcome, superiority, ranking, patent, clinical-study, survival-rate, numerical, comparative, celebrity, or regulatory claims.
