# DUOLAC Thailand — Brand Background

## Working description

DUOLAC is a Korean probiotic brand being prepared for the Thailand market. The Thailand marketing system should communicate premium quality, science-led product development, and trustworthy everyday gut-health support.

## Positioning direction

- Premium Korean probiotic brand
- Science-led and evidence-conscious
- Clean, modern, refined, and trustworthy
- Accessible enough for everyday wellness communication
- Thai-market localization with international brand cues

## Tone of voice

Use: precise, calm, premium, reassuring, science-aware, human.

Avoid: exaggerated promises, fear-based health messaging, miracle language, aggressive urgency, unsupported superlatives, and diagnosis or treatment claims.

## Visual direction

- Clean premium layout
- Strong product focus
- Generous whitespace
- Brand colors first; product-specific accent colors second
- High legibility on mobile marketplace thumbnails
- Avoid clutter, unverified badges, and visual claims that are not supported by text evidence

## Customer-facing language

Default to Thai for marketplace and social content. Keep technical terms in English only when they are part of the approved product naming or improve clarity.
