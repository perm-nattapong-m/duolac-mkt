- Official product name and SKU
- Packaging photos and approved logo usage

- Thai registration/label information where applicable
- Approved claims and supporting source
- Target customer and channel priority
- Price, bundle, and promotion rules

## Product copy rule

The LLM may express an approved fact in clearer language, but may not increase its strength, add a medical outcome, or imply guaranteed results.
