# Shopee and Lazada Mall Content Rules

This file is a working checklist. Replace assumptions with current official platform rules before publishing.

## Listing content checklist

- Product title is accurate and searchable without keyword stuffing.
- Product images match the exact SKU and pack size.
- Price, quantity, promotion, and bundle information are current.
- Claims are supported by `docs/claims/claim-register.md`.
- Thai copy is natural and understandable on mobile.
- Required warnings, usage instructions, storage, and seller information are included where applicable.
- No unsupported medical, guaranteed, or misleading claims.

## Image-set planning

1. Hero image: product and clear category/identity.
2. Product facts: pack size, format, and key approved information.
3. Why it matters: educational explanation without diagnosis or fear.
4. How to use: serving and storage instructions.
5. Trust/evidence: only verified badges, certifications, or research claims.
6. Brand/store: official-store and customer-support information.

## Platform adaptation

Create a separate brief for each platform. Do not assume that one image size, text density, or claim policy works everywhere.

Record for every asset:

- Platform and placement
- Canvas size/aspect ratio
- Maximum text density
- SKU
- Version and date
- Approval status
