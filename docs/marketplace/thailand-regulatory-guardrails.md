# Thailand Regulatory Guardrails for DUOLAC Content

Research date: 2026-08-25
Status: operational guardrail, not legal advice. Every label, listing, advertisement and influencer script requires final Thai regulatory/legal review.

## Why this matters

Thailand’s FDA Food Division maintains specific rules and guidance for probiotic microorganisms in foods. Its referenced lists contain named microorganisms for food use; a microorganism outside the listed groups requires a safety-evaluation process. Therefore, a global DUOLAC formula must be reviewed strain-by-strain before Thai import, label, or marketing use.

The Thai FDA also publicly enforces against food-supplement advertising that makes unapproved or misleading therapeutic/health claims. Disease-treatment language is not allowed for food supplements.

## Mandatory content gate

Before publishing any Thai listing, image, caption, ad, creator brief or customer-service macro, confirm:

- Exact Thailand SKU and Thai food registration/label status
- Full strain names and formula match the approved/imported version
- Dose, CFU, storage and age/use information match the approved label
- Every explicit or implied benefit is in docs/claims/claim-register.md
- Any numerical study, patent, certification, ranking, “No.1”, “221×”, “clinically proven”, doctor/pharmacist endorsement, or comparison has approval plus source
- Required disclaimer and Thai-language label copy are correct

## Red-flag claims: block by default

- Treats, prevents, cures, or relieves a disease or symptom
- IBS, infection, diarrhea treatment, cancer, depression, inflammation, hormone/PCOS, weight loss, cholesterol, detox or “heals the gut”
- “Guaranteed” result, “works for everyone,” or direct before/after efficacy
- “FDA approved” (unless legally exact and appropriate; US GRAS is not Thai FDA product approval)
- “Doctor recommended” or “pharmacist recommended” without documented, locally usable substantiation
- Claims based only on a strain, ingredient, foreign pack, or research pipeline rather than the exact Thai product

## Copy approach that is safer pending approval

Prefer quality/process education:
- Korean-developed probiotic brand
- product details exactly as stated on the Thai label
- “Designed with Dual Coating technology” once documentation and permitted wording are approved
- “Read label / consult a healthcare professional where appropriate”

Avoid outcome certainty. Do not create a health claim by pairing an otherwise neutral sentence with a disease image, symptom question, or before/after visual.
