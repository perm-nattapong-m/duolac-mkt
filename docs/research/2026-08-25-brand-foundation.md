# DUOLAC Brand Foundation — Research Pack

Research date: 2026-08-25
Purpose: initial source-of-truth context for Thai-market marketing, product, and design generation. This is internal research, not approved customer-facing copy.

## Executive read

DUOLAC is the consumer probiotic brand of South Korea's Cell Biotech. The brand's strongest defensible differentiation is not a generic “high CFU” message; it is a proprietary protein-and-polysaccharide Dual Coating platform intended to protect viable bacteria through storage and digestion. The company combines this platform with internally developed CBT strains, in-house manufacturing, and pharmacist/health-professional-led distribution in several markets.

For Thailand, the promising positioning is:

> Premium K-probiotic whose quality story is “what reaches the gut,” not merely the label CFU count.

Use this as a strategic direction, not a health claim.

## Brand communication assets

### Korean campaign / celebrity

Cell Biotech announced a Korean DUOLAC campaign on 2025-09-10 featuring actor Son Suk-ku. The campaign headline centers on “221 Times Stronger Probiotics” and links the message to Dual Coating and gut survivability.

Use in Thailand: This is evidence of the parent brand's creative direction and tone only. Do not use Son Suk-ku’s name, image, campaign artwork, slogan, or imply endorsement in Thailand without written regional usage rights.

### Channel pattern

The brand's public material repeatedly uses pharmacies, clinics, e-commerce, professional seminars, factory tours, and health-professional education. That supports a Thai launch model in which marketplace sales are paired with clinical-quality education rather than discount-first messaging.

## Approved working vocabulary

Use: Korean-developed probiotic strains; patented Dual Coating technology; premium probiotic; science-led; quality-focused; daily gut-health routine; viability through the digestive journey.

Avoid: cure; treat; prevent; detox; guaranteed results; “works for everyone”; “the best”; “clinically proven” without a specific product/study linkage; superiority over all probiotics.
