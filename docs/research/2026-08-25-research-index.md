## Evidence hierarchy

1. Thai approved label / registration / written legal clearance
2. Exact product technical dossier and permitted claim documentation
3. Peer-reviewed human clinical study of the exact formulation
4. Official company source with auditable primary support
5. Patent / regulatory record — supports ownership or status, not efficacy
6. Secondary articles and market commentary

## Retrieval prompt

When generating any marketplace content:

> Read the brand foundation, relevant product catalog record, claim register, Thai regulatory guardrails, and applicable research note. State the SKU and output platform. Use only claims marked verified/restricted with their permitted wording. Mark missing facts as [NEEDS VERIFICATION]. Separate factual copy from creative options.
