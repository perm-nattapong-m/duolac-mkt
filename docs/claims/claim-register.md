# Claim Register

Use this register before any claim appears in a product image, listing, caption, video, or advertisement.

| Claim | Category | Status | Evidence/source | Allowed wording | Restricted wording | Reviewer | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: Korean origin | brand fact | pending_verification | Add official source | To be approved | Do not imply clinical superiority |  | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Status definitions

- `verified`: supported by an official or approved source and cleared for the relevant channel.
- `pending_verification`: research lead only; do not publish.
- `restricted`: usable only with specific wording, context, or disclaimer.
- `not_for_publication`: internal reference; never use in generated customer-facing content.

## High-risk claim types

Always require review for medical, disease-related, guaranteed-outcome, superiority, ranking, patent, clinical-study, survival-rate, numerical, comparative, or regulatory claims.
